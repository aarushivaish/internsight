About InternSight

Built by students,
for students.

InternSight started as a frustration and became a platform. We saw how much time peers wasted hunting across dozens of portals — and decided to automate the entire process with Django, smart scraping, and a clean interface.

Our Mission

Opportunity should find you.

The average student spends 5 to 7 hours every week manually browsing internship portals — refreshing pages, cross-referencing deadlines, and maintaining spreadsheets just to stay on top of available roles.

We built InternSight to fix that. By aggregating listings from 20+ sources into a single, intelligently filtered dashboard, we give students those hours back — so they can spend time actually applying, preparing, and growing.

Powered by Django, SqlLite3, and custom web scrapers, InternSight runs autonomously — fetching, deduplicating, and surfacing the most relevant opportunities in real time.

"Automating Opportunity Discovery Through Scalable Django Engineering."
— InternSight, founding principle
Technology

How it's built

A pragmatic, production-grade stack chosen for reliability, speed, and extensibility.

Django
Backend framework handling business logic, ORM, admin interface, and management commands for automated scraping.
SqlLite3
Reliable relational storage with unique constraints to eliminate duplicates and maintain a clean dataset.
Scraper Layer
serpAPI
REST API
Django REST Framework powers structured endpoints for all frontend data access, with filtering, pagination, and authentication.
